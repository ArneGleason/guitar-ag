# Contributing

Issues, testing notes, pull requests, MIDI audition files, documentation fixes, and DSP experiments are welcome.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to submit it and that it may be included in Guitar AG under the project's current license. Please keep the license and required notices with redistributed copies or forks.

The most useful contributions right now are practical DAW reports, clear reproduction steps, focused code changes, and short before/after listening notes.
